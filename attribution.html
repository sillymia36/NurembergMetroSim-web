Data sources

  • Track, station and scenery data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Geometry is adapted and simplified.
  • Terrain and scenery analysis: DGM1 elevation data and DOP40 orthophotos, Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung, CC BY 4.0. Datenquelle: Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung - www.geodaten.bayern.de. Data was resampled or analysed; the original imagery is not included.
  • Siding and crossover layouts were informed by Christian Stade's unofficial Gleisplanweb schematic, identified as CC BY-NC-SA. The original artwork is not included; simulated layouts are approximate.
  • Track elevation profiles were approximately transcribed from city-credited longitudinal-profile drawings published by Nahverkehr Franken. The source drawings are not included.

Alpha limitations

Routes, elevations, signalling and infrastructure are provisional and not an operational track plan. The browser build requires WebAssembly and WebGL 2.0; terrain loading may cause pauses. Leitstelle / Funk networking is unavailable in browsers.

Use the on-screen controls with touch or mouse; keyboard controls are also available.
